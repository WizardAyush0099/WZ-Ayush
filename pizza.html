Our Pabbar Valley Story

From Himalayan Hills to Wood-Fired Perfection

Pizza Town Rohru was founded with a singular conviction: that the pure mountain atmosphere, mineral-rich Pabbar waters, and seasonal produce of Himachal Pradesh deserved to meet the timeless culinary discipline of Neapolitan pizza making.

Every morning our pizzaiolos blend Italian Tipo 00 flour with pristine spring water for a 48-hour cold fermentation. Baked rapidly in our stone oven fired with aromatic mountain pine wood, every pie develops an airy, blistered crust with unmatched aroma.
