Method & Mastery

The Slow Bar Extraction Ritual

Every pour-over at Velvet Brew is orchestrated through calibrated weights, precise hydrodynamic flow, and timed extraction stages.

01.

Origin & Burr Calibration

Single-dose weighed to 0.05g accuracy and milled via 98mm cast-steel flat burrs at 420 microns for razor-sharp uniformity.

02.

The 45-Second Bloom

Triple dry-weight saturated with 93°C volcanic remineralized water, gently degassing trapped carbon dioxide without agitating fines.

03.

Concentric Spiral Extraction

Steady 5.2g/s flow through goose-neck copper kettle to maintain stable thermal slurry, teasing out delicate stone-fruit aromatics.

04.

Wheel-Thrown Presentation

Decanted into custom Japanese unglazed ceramic vessels engineered to concentrate aromatics as the elixir cools naturally.
