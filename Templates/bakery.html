The Levain Philosophy

THE ART OF THE
4:00 AM RISE

Founded along the winding cobblestones of Paris and perfected locally, CRUMB & CO. was born from an obsession with unhurried time. We believe real bread cannot be rushed.

Every loaf begins forty-eight hours before it meets the oven. We mill our heirloom wheat on French granite stones to preserve the wheat germ’s living nutrients, pairing it with our hundred-year-old sourdough starter. The result is an airy, custard-like crumb protected by a blistered, caramelized crust that speaks of deep earth and fire.

OUR CRAFT: FOUR PILLARS OF EXCELLENCE

True Viennoiserie requires reverence for the earth, time-tested physical metallurgy, and microscopic natural fermentation.

grain
Pillar I

Stone-Milled Heritage Grains

Single-origin organic French wheat and ancient Rouge de Bordeaux grains, freshly ground on French granite stones to retain every live enzyme.

bubble_chart
Pillar II

The 100-Year Levain

A living sourdough starter nurtured continuously since 1924, fed twice daily with pure mountain spring water and unbleached rye.

water_drop
Pillar III

AOP Normandy Butter

84% minimum butterfat Beurre de Tourage from grass-fed cows in Isigny-sur-Mer, creating distinct honeycomb lamination without greasiness.

fireplace
Pillar IV

Volcanic Stone Deck Ovens

Our bakes rest directly on custom French volcanic stone decks, producing high thermal inertia and steam-injected caramelized crusts.
